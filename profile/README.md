## Engagement Tracks

| Track | What ROBOCO brings |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Bootcamp** | Practical AI training, vibe-coding workshops, workflow setup, and first MVP support for teams getting started. |
| **AI Partnership** | Hands-on pairing for automation, legacy modernization, software delivery, and internal AI champion development. |
| **AI Transformation Advisory** | Executive-level roadmap design, org-wide enablement, and AI integration across management, operations, and product. |

## Operating Model

- **Teach, don't trap.** ROBOCO helps client teams build internal capability instead of becoming dependent on outsourcing.
- **Guardrails over hype.** Safe adoption matters as much as speed, especially around hallucination risk, security, and technical debt.
- **From strategy to shipping.** Consulting, education, and hands-on development are treated as one connected system.
